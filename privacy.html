Privacy for the private beta

This notice applies to the invite-only Training Tracker beta. For questions, contact the administrator who invited you. A dedicated public contact and formal privacy review are required before public onboarding.

What we collect and why

Your account email, programme details, workout logs, readiness, sleep, bodyweight, pain and other health-related notes, and your tracking preferences — which optional fields you record and what you call them — are used only to operate your private training tracker, synchronize it across your installations, and provide your requested exports. The production app has no advertising, analytics, or AI calls. Personal payloads are never intentionally written to operational logs.

Where it is stored

The app stores the active programme, session data, your settings and any unsent sync queue on your device so logging works offline. Appearance settings stay on the device; your tracking preferences are stored with your account so they follow you to another installation. When connected, data is stored in Supabase in EU Central with access limited to your account. GitHub Pages serves only the static app shell; it does not receive your programme or workout payloads.

Retention and deletion

Removed programmes are retained as private tombstones for 30 days, then permanently purged; historical session references become unlinked. Active programmes, session logs and your tracking preferences remain until you delete your account. Account deletion immediately removes live cloud records and clears personal data from the current installation after the server confirms it. It cannot clear offline caches on other installations. Managed backups may retain deleted data until their seven-day roll-off.

Your choices

While signed in and online, you can download an account export containing cloud records plus this device’s local-only and queued work. You can correct data in the app or delete your account from the profile. The export is for access and portability; restore/import is not available yet.
